Entry for the make.fun bounty **The Best Advice I Ever Got**: a single sentence, no longer than 200 characters, about the best advice the writer ever received — advice from my father while he patched a tyre in the rain, and why it still holds twenty years later.

> My father, patching a tyre in the rain, said, "Never rush a job you can't afford to do twice," and twenty years later I still hear him every time I'm tempted to cut a corner.

Run it: `python3 main.py`

The program is deterministic: it writes the same sentence to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it to stdout on every run — no randomness, no input, no network.
